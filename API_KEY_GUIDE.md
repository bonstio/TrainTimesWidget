# How to Get a Free API Key for Train Times

To show live UK train departures on your widget, you need a free API key from the official **Rail Data Marketplace**. 

It is an easy **3-step process**:

---

### Step 1: Create a Free Account
1. Go to the [Rail Data Marketplace](https://raildata.org.uk/).
2. Click **Sign up** / **Register** and create a free account.
3. Wait for your account approval email (approval usually takes between 12 to 24 hours).

---

### Step 2: Subscribe to Live Departure Boards
1. Once logged in, search for the **Live Departure Board** product.
2. Click the **Subscribe** button (it is 100% free).

---

### Step 3: Copy Your Key & Paste into the App
1. On the Live Departure Board product page, click the **Specification** tab to view your **API Key** (*Consumer Key* / *Primary Key*).
2. Copy the key.
3. Open the **Train Times** app, paste the key into the API key box, and tap **Save**.

---

That's it! Your widgets and commute notifications are now ready to show live UK train times.
